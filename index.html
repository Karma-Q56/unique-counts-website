Welcome to Unique Counts

Founded in 2025, UNIQUE COUNTS (Pty) Ltd has quickly established itself as a trusted name in the stone fabrication and installation industry across Pretoria and surrounding areas.

Our Story

UNIQUE COUNTS began with limited resources but with a bold vision to make a lasting impact in the granite industry. Early challenges became the driving force behind our innovation, pushing us to adopt smarter, more efficient methods in granite processing and installation.

Through dedication, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to excellence, we've built a reputation for quality craftsmanship and exceptional service. Our motto, "Look – Touch – Feel the Gem," reflects the culture and standard that guides every member of our team.

Our Expertise

We specialize in the installation of custom stonework including kitchen countertops, coffee tables, window sills, bathroom vanities, staircases, and tombstones. Our materials include granite, Caesarstone, marble, quartz, and mozaiek—each selected for durability and aesthetic appeal.
