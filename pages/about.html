About Unique Counts

Look – Touch – Feel the Gem. Discover the story, values, and team behind Pretoria's premier stone craftsmanship.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, UNIQUE COUNTS began with limited human, material, and financial resources but with a bold vision to make a lasting impact in the granite industry across all 9 provinces of South Africa — Gauteng, Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, North West, Free State, Northern Cape, and the Eastern Cape.

Early challenges became the driving force behind our innovation, pushing us to adopt smarter, more efficient methods in granite processing and installation. Through dedication, resilience, and a commitment to excellence, we've built a reputation for quality and exceptional service.

Our motto, "Look – Touch – Feel the Gem," is more than a slogan—it reflects the culture and standard that guides every member of our team.

Procedures & Working Standards

UNIQUE COUNTS has been a driving force in the stone fabrication and installation industry for over seven years.

During this time, the company has built and maintained an exceptional reputation among its clients and peers in the same trade. This strong rapport is a testament to the company's unwavering commitment to quality, reliability, and client satisfaction.

Every thriving business defines its own standards and procedures to ensure excellence, and UNIQUE COUNTS is no exception. Its success is not the result of mere chance or luck, but the outcome of dedication, vision, and consistent delivery.

Our Specializations

The company specializes in a wide range of services including stone fabrication, epoxy flooring, and waterproofing:

  • Kitchen countertops
  • Coffee tables
  • Window sills
  • Bathroom vanities
  • Staircases
  • Epoxy flooring
  • Waterproofing (roofs, slabs, walls & more)

Premium Materials

These masterpieces are crafted from premium materials such as granite, Caesarstone, marble, quartz, and mozaiek—each selected for its durability and aesthetic appeal.

These stones originate from nature's depths—mountains, lakes, and oceans—while others, like Caesarstone, are meticulously engineered and imported to meet the highest design standards.
