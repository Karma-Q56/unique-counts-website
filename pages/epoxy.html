Epoxy Flooring Features

The advantages that make epoxy the preferred choice for discerning property owners.

Exceptional Durability

Withstands heavy foot traffic, machinery, and impact without cracking, chipping, or wearing down for years.

Chemical Resistance

Resistant to oils, solvents, acids, and chemicals—ideal for garages, workshops, and industrial facilities.

Easy Maintenance

Seamless, non-porous surface is incredibly easy to clean—just sweep and mop for a spotless floor.

Design Versatility

Available in unlimited colors, patterns, and finishes including metallic, flake, and quartz systems.

Moisture Resistant

Creates a waterproof barrier that protects concrete substrates from moisture damage and mold.

Safety Options

Anti-slip additives available for wet areas, plus options for static-dissipative and non-conductive floors.

Epoxy System Options

Choose the perfect epoxy system for your specific needs.

Self-Leveling Epoxy

The most popular choice for creating smooth, seamless surfaces over old or new concrete. Perfect for showrooms, garages, and commercial spaces.

  • Thickness: 2-3mm
  • Durability: Excellent
  • Finish: High-gloss, smooth

Epoxy Flake System

Decorative vinyl flakes broadcast into epoxy create a beautiful, textured surface with excellent slip resistance and hide imperfections.

  • Thickness: 3-5mm
  • Durability: Excellent
  • Finish: Textured, matte

Metallic Epoxy

Create stunning, three-dimensional effects with metallic pigments that flow and swirl to form unique patterns. Perfect for luxury spaces.

  • Thickness: 2-3mm
  • Durability: Excellent
  • Finish: High-gloss, decorative

Quartz Epoxy System

Colored quartz aggregates combined with epoxy create an extremely durable, slip-resistant surface ideal for wet areas and heavy traffic.

  • Thickness: 5-8mm
  • Durability: Exceptional
  • Finish: Textured, anti-slip

Our Process

Professional installation ensuring flawless, long-lasting results.

1

Surface Assessment

We evaluate your concrete substrate, checking for moisture, cracks, and contamination that need to be addressed.

2

Surface Preparation

Diamond grinding and shot blasting create the perfect profile for epoxy adhesion. Repairs are made to cracks and damaged areas.

3

Primer Application

A penetrating primer seals the concrete and ensures strong bonding between the substrate and epoxy coating.

4

Epoxy Installation & Curing

Multiple layers of premium epoxy are applied with proper cure times between coats. Final topcoat seals and protects.
