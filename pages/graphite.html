Why Graphite Work Matters

Graphite is one of the most versatile materials in industrial and architectural applications. Its unique properties make it ideal for surfaces that require exceptional heat resistance, low friction, and lasting durability.

At Unique Counts, we specialize in professional graphite work that combines functional excellence with aesthetic appeal. Whether you need industrial-grade surfaces or decorative elements with a sophisticated metallic finish, our graphite solutions deliver unmatched performance.

Key Features

Our graphite work delivers exceptional performance across a wide range of applications.

Heat Resistance

Withstands temperatures exceeding 500°C, making it perfect for fireplace surrounds, braai areas, and industrial applications.

Durability

Exceptionally hard surface that resists scratches, impact, and wear, maintaining its appearance for decades.

Low Maintenance

Non-porous surface that repels stains and is easy to clean with just soap and water.

Chemical Resistance

Resistant to most chemicals, acids, and solvents, ideal for laboratory and industrial environments.

Electrical Conductivity

Naturally conductive properties make it suitable for specialized electrical and electronic applications.

Aesthetic Appeal

Sophisticated metallic finish with subtle texture that adds modern elegance to any space.

Where Graphite Excels

Our graphite solutions are perfect for both residential and industrial applications.

Fireplace Surrounds

Bbq & Braai Areas

Industrial Facilities

Laboratories

Decorative Accents

Modern wall panels, countertops, and architectural features with industrial-chic appeal.

Mechanical Components

Bearings, seals, and components requiring low friction and high wear resistance.
